Privacy

What nitsdate stores, who can see it, and how to get it removed. Updated 22 November 2026.

What is stored

  • From Google, when you sign in: your name, email address, Google account ID and the basic profile details Google sends with them (such as a link to your Google profile picture). Nothing else is requested: no contacts, no Drive, no permission to post for you. Google sign-in tokens are not kept.
  • What you add: profile answers, date of birth, gender, photos, posts, comments, votes, messages and chat photos, connections, blocks and reports.
  • Technical: a sign-in cookie and a security (CSRF) cookie. Your light/dark choice and whether sounds are on stay in your own browser. The time you were last on the site: matchmaking uses it to pair people who are around first, moderators can see it, and your Priority chats see it as "Active now" or "Active 5 minutes ago". Nobody else does. The server's access logs record IP addresses and the pages requested; they are deleted after 14 days.

No ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when a page loads.

All traffic passes through Cloudflare, which protects the site from attacks and keeps the server's address private. Like any such network, Cloudflare decrypts and re-encrypts traffic on its way to the server, so it handles your IP address and what you send and receive. It has no access to the database.

Who can see what

  • Which NIT you're at comes from your college email address and is shown on your profile.
  • Campus feed posts, and the comments under them, are seen only by students of your NIT. {% if ALL_NITS_LIVE %}Posts you send to the All NITs feed are seen by students of every NIT, with your NIT next to your name.{% else %}An All NITs feed is coming; posts there will be seen by students of every NIT.{% endif %}
  • You choose for each answer and each photo: only me, connections, or everyone signed in. "Everyone signed in" includes students of other NITs who open your profile.
  • You can show a random name instead of your real one, and lock your profile so strangers see only your randomized name.
  • In a Priority chat you both see when the other was last active, when they're typing, and whether they've seen your latest message. Other chats show none of this, and blocking messages hides it.
  • In matchmaking you both get blind nicknames until you connect. You're matched within your own NIT{% if ALL_NITS_LIVE %} unless you both choose All NITs{% else %}; matching across NITs is coming{% endif %}.
  • Notifications tell you when someone upvotes, comments on or replies to what you posted. Upvote notifications never say who voted. You can switch each kind off on the notifications page.
  • Apart from this page, the terms and the sign-in page, everything needs a sign-in, and search engines are told not to index the site.
  • Moderators (a small team chosen by the admins, for every NIT) can see an account's email address, every profile answer and photo whatever its privacy setting, and its posts and comments. When a message or chat is reported they can read up to 100 messages before the report, but nothing after it. Every moderator action is logged. Whoever you report is never told who reported them.

Photos

Photos are re-encoded on upload, which removes location (GPS) and camera data. They are kept in a private Cloudflare R2 storage bucket in the EU and only shown through the site, after checking that the viewer is allowed to see them.

Where it lives

The site and its database run on a server in Frankfurt, Germany, reached through Cloudflare. Database backups are kept for 14 days.

Under 18

You must be at least 16. Members aged 16 or 17 are only matched with each other. Your date of birth is required for this, and only a moderator can correct it.

Deleting things

You can delete your posts, comments and photos at any time. To delete your whole account and everything in it, use Delete my account at the bottom of your profile page{% if contact_email %}, or email {{ contact_email }} from the address you sign in with{% endif %}. It is removed from the site straight away, and drops out of backups within 14 days. Only your email address and date of birth are kept, with any limits a moderator had set, so that signing up again with the same email brings back the same date of birth and limits.
