How it works

  1. You're paired with someone who fits you both: who you're each looking for, age range and any filters you switched on. Under-18s are only ever matched with other under-18s.
  2. You chat under made-up nicknames. No profiles, no real names.
  3. After {{ connect_after }} messages each, a Connect button appears. When they accept, the chat moves to your Inbox and your profiles unlock.
  4. You're never matched with someone you're already connected with, and the same two people won't be matched again for {{ cooldown_days }} days.
